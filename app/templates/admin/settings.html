Formula Comparison Guide

1. Standard Addition (Default):

Final % = (Attended + Medical) / Total × 100
Directly adds approved medical classes to the student's attended count.

2. Duty Leave / Exemption:

Final % = Attended / (Total - Medical) × 100
Excuses medical hours entirely, reducing the denominator required.

3. Capped Benefit:

Regular % + min(Medical %, Max Cap)
Prevents students from passing solely on medical certificates if regular attendance was excessively low.

4. Weighted Factor:

(Attended + (Medical × Weight)) / Total × 100
Applies partial credit (e.g. 75% or 50% benefit) to medical absences.
